Runnable map guide

First-time use: a map can be run in ACT Lab only when the required pieces are present.

    Required

    • Place 1 mouse on a tile and set its facing with ↑ ← → ↓.
    • Place 1 EXIT on a different tile (the goal).
    • Mouse, EXIT, and cakes cannot share the same tile.

    Recommended

    • Draw walls on grid lines to form a maze; walls do not occupy tile interiors.
    • Cakes are optional. The mouse does not know the full map; it eats cakes only after seeing them, then looks for EXIT.
    • Leave a path from the mouse to EXIT; do not wall it in completely.
    • If you place extra mice or EXITs, the run uses only the first of each.

    Controls

    • 1 wall (drag to set length) · 2 mouse · 3 EXIT · 4 cake · 5 select (drag a box)
    • Hold Shift to pan the map. Right-click deletes an item.
    • Save JSON, then open it in ACT Lab and press Run.
